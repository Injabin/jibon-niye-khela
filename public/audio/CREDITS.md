## Sound effects

All SFX (`SFX_MANIFEST` in `lib/audio/manifest.ts`) are **procedurally
synthesized** at runtime with the Web Audio API (`lib/audio/synth.ts`). No
files, samples, or third-party licensing apply.

Rule (AGENT.md §9): only CC0/CC-BY audio, or audio synthesized locally. Any
new asset must be logged here with its license and attribution before it is
bundled.
